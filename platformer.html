Cross-Device Platformer

A responsive platformer game that works on all devices

Score: 0
Lives: 3
Level: 1
Move: A D or ← → Jump: SPACE or W or ↑

How to Play

Guide the character through platforms, collect coins, and reach the flag to complete each level!

  • Avoid red enemies - they will reduce your lives
  • Collect golden coins - each coin gives you 10 points
  • Reach the checkered flag to advance to the next level
  • Don't fall off the platforms or you'll lose a life

On mobile/tablet: Use touch buttons On computer: Use keyboard controls
