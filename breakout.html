HOW TO PLAY

Move Paddle - Use mouse/touch or arrow keys to move the paddle left and right
Bounce Ball - Keep the ball in play by hitting it with the paddle
Break Bricks - Destroy all bricks to complete the level and earn points
Collect Power-ups - Some bricks drop power-ups that give special abilities
On mobile/tablet: Use touch buttons or tilt device
On computer: Use ← → or A D or Mouse
