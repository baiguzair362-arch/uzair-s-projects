HOW TO PLAY

  • Goal: Whack as many moles as possible before time runs out!
  • Normal Moles: Click them to score 10 points.
  • Golden Moles: Rare and worth 30 points!
  • Bomb Moles: Avoid these! They end the game immediately.
  • Scoring: You get bonus points for consecutive hits.
  • Difficulty: Higher difficulties have faster moles and more bomb moles.
  • Game Over: When time runs out or you hit a bomb mole.
