How to Play

  • Click on any card to flip it over and reveal the icon.
  • Click on a second card to try to find the matching icon.
  • If the two cards match, they will remain face up.
  • If they don't match, they will flip back over after a short delay.
  • The game ends when you find all matching pairs.
  • Try to complete the game in the shortest time with the fewest moves!

Difficulty Levels

  • Easy: 6 pairs, 60 seconds timer
  • Medium: 8 pairs, 45 seconds timer
  • Hard: 12 pairs, 30 seconds timer
